# Booking an appointment
New and existing patients can book by email or phone. Tell us what the visit is for (check-up, pain, hygiene, whitening) and two or three preferred days; reception confirms a time.

# First visit
A first visit takes about 30 minutes and includes a check-up and, if needed, a panoramic X-ray. Please bring your ID and any recent X-rays.

# Visit length
Check-ups and hygiene visits take 30 minutes. Fillings take 45–60 minutes. Whitening takes 90 minutes.
