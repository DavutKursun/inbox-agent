# Before your visit
Eat normally unless we told you otherwise. Brush your teeth before coming. If you take blood thinners or have a heart condition, tell us when booking.

# Children
We see children from age 4. A parent or guardian must be present for patients under 18.

# Pain or swelling
If you have swelling, fever or pain that keeps you awake, say so in your message: we keep same-day slots for urgent cases.
