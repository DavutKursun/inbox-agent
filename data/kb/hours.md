# Opening hours
Nova Dental Clinic is open Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 18:00 and on Saturday from 10:00 to 14:00. We are closed on Sundays and on national public holidays.

# Saturday
Saturday appointments are for check-ups and hygiene visits only; longer treatments are scheduled on weekdays.

# Emergencies
For dental emergencies outside opening hours, call the emergency line listed on your appointment confirmation. Email is not monitored outside opening hours.
