# Invoices
An e-invoice (e-Fatura) is emailed within 24 hours of each visit. You can ask reception for a copy at any time; include the visit date in your request.

# Outstanding balances
If a payment is outstanding you will receive a reminder by email. Balances can be paid at reception or by bank transfer; the IBAN is printed on the invoice.

# Disputes
If you believe an invoice is wrong, reply to the invoice email and a member of staff will review it within three working days. Refunds are decided by the clinic manager, not by email.
