# Cancellation policy
Appointments can be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to 24 hours before the appointment time. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice, and missed appointments, are charged ₺500.

# How to cancel
Reply to your confirmation email or call reception. Cancellations by email are confirmed by a member of staff; they are not automatic.

# Late arrival
If you arrive more than 15 minutes late we may need to shorten or reschedule your visit.
