# Address
Nova Dental Clinic, Bağdat Caddesi 210, Kadıköy, Istanbul. Second floor; there is a lift.

# Parking
There is no clinic car park. The nearest public car park is Kadıköy Belediyesi İSPARK on Şaşkınbakkal, a 3-minute walk. Street parking on Bağdat Caddesi is limited to 2 hours.

# Public transport
The Suadiye Marmaray station is a 10-minute walk; buses 4, 16 and 19 stop in front of the building.
