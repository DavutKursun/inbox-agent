# Price list — what treatments cost
All prices are in Turkish lira and include VAT. These are the fees for standard treatments.

- Check-up and consultation: ₺900
- Hygiene visit (scale and polish): ₺1,500
- Composite filling (per tooth): ₺2,000 – ₺3,500 depending on size
- Teeth whitening (in-clinic): ₺6,000
- Panoramic X-ray: ₺700

# Estimates
Prices for crowns, implants and orthodontics are given after an examination; we send a written estimate by email before any treatment starts.

# Payment
We accept cash, debit and credit cards. Treatment plans above ₺10,000 can be paid in three instalments with no interest.
